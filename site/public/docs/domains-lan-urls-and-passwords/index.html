Domains, LAN URLs and passwords

Every app gets two addresses:

URL Works from
https://myapp.example.com anywhere, through the Cloudflare tunnel
https://myapp.your-mac.local your LAN, advertised over Bonjour, with Caddy's local certificate

your-mac is the Mac's local hostname (System Settings → General → Sharing). To get a padlock on LAN URLs on the Mac itself, run berth trust-ca. On your other devices, install ~/.berth/caddy/pki/authorities/local/root.crt as a trusted certificate.

Custom domains

berth domains:add myapp www.mysite.com
berth domains:list myapp
berth domains:remove myapp www.mysite.com

Anything under your main domain just works. For a domain in another zone on your Cloudflare account, log in to that zone once (it opens the browser) and then add it:

berth cf:login mysite.com
berth domains:add myapp www.mysite.com
berth domains:add myapp mysite.com          # the bare domain works too

If you added a domain before its zone was connected, run the same domains:add again after cf:login to create its DNS record.

Berth creates the DNS record and updates the tunnel live. It won't replace a record that's already there (say www points at your old host) unless you add --force. If the domain's DNS lives somewhere else, domains:add prints the record to create (www.mysite.com CNAME <tunnel-id>.cfargotunnel.com), but the zone still has to be proxied through Cloudflare for the tunnel to serve it.

LAN-only apps and passwords

berth create admin-tool --repo you/admin-tool --private   # LAN only, no public URL
berth auth:set myapp alice:correct-horse-battery          # HTTP basic auth, enforced by Caddy
berth auth:unset myapp
